Build the two pure crypto primitives Phase 10 depends on: `crypto/shamir.js` (Shamir 2-of-3 split/reconstruct of the 32-byte DEK, CUST-01 / D-01) and `crypto/rsaShare.js` (RSA-2048-OAEP-SHA256 wrap/unwrap of a single share, D-04).
